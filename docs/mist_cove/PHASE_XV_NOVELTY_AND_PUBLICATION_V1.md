# Phase XV novelty and publication position

The defensible candidate is **exact, dependency-aware auditing of one EEG classifier's behavior under a declared internal withholding operator**, accompanied by separate signal-domain and automated-event-proxy evaluations. This is a combined engineering and empirical methods claim, not a claim that sufficient subsets, Boolean explanations, or time-series attribution were invented here. The current ST evidence is exploratory and the sigma-power baseline outperforms exact-positive support on the registered spindle-like proxy.

Known foundations and their implications:

| Prior work | Established idea | Phase XV distinction and limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Carter et al., 2019, *Sufficient Input Subsets*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/carter19a/carter19a.pdf) | Minimal retained subsets can keep a model response after other inputs are masked. | Retention alone is not novel. Our 15-atom exhaustive table also exposes withheld, interfering, alternative, and prior-only states under exact overlap closure. The result is specific to this operator and model. |
| [Ignatiev et al., 2020, abductive and contrastive explanations](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/from-contrastive-to-abductive-explanations-and-back-again/) | Formal sufficient and contrastive explanations have established relationships and enumeration methods. | Signed clauses and counterexamples cannot be claimed as first-in-kind logic. Our contribution is an EEG-specific, independently checked application with explicit physical/operator boundaries. |
| [Bryant, 1986, ordered decision diagrams](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/classes/cs289/f04/readings/Bryant%3A1986.pdf) | Reduced ordered binary decision diagrams are a canonical representation for a fixed variable order. | The diagram itself is standard. We use it to verify and summarize all 32,768 states, including nonmonotone response regions. |
| [Enguehard, 2023, learned time-series perturbations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/enguehard23a/enguehard23a.pdf) | Time-series explanations already use preservation and deletion perturbations. | Our exact finite response table is a different, bounded internal operator; it does not solve the realism or clinical validity of waveform replacement. |
| [Vallat and Walker, 2021, YASA](https://elifesciences.org/articles/70092) | Open-source sleep staging and event-analysis tools provide automated physiological candidates. | Our detector is a fixed stage-blind proxy, not an expert adjudication target. Method rankings never read its positions before selecting intervals. |
| [Wendt et al., 2015, spindle scoring reliability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4426257/) | Expert spindle annotations have material inter- and intra-rater variability. | Independent, blinded expert labels and adjudication are needed before claiming human-relevant event localization. |
| [PhysioNet Sleep-EDF Expanded](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/sleep-cassette/) and [single-lead cross-domain staging study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10796543/) | ST's paired placebo/temazepam nights and domain/channel mismatch are known, and transfer can degrade staging. | Phase XV characterizes one authenticated frozen source classifier; it cannot claim generalizable REM detection or treatment effects. |
| [Multimodal electrophysiology modality-importance study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10050434/) | Sleep-Telemetry multimodal and modality-importance analyses already exist. | Merely comparing EEG/EOG/EMG or calling EOG informative is not a new claim. Our internal circuit/physical/proxy separation requires stronger direct comparator studies to become a publishable methodological advance. |

The quantitative case for model-dependency auditing is sound but narrow: all 360 new original-response tables were checked exhaustively, 187/219 representative E1 cases were nonmonotone, and a mild nuisance view retained the predicted class in 93/100 cases while the same primary clause remained valid in only 70/100. There are real alternatives and interfering contexts worth reporting. However, 66/219 E1 cases require whole-epoch positive support, 19/219 have a prior response, neutral-state changes affect 190/219 tables, and physical replacements are often incompatible. These facts constrain the natural-language explanation one can attach to a clause.

The quantitative case for superior physiological event localization is currently negative: the prior registered ST exact-minus-sigma gate failed, and the new 55-case, 20-person exploratory spindle-like proxy contrast is −0.0462 [−0.0797, −0.0167]. A favorable comparison to random, RMS, delta, or a particular gradient ranking does not establish superiority over the best simple relevant baseline. The event target is automated and unvalidated. No human reviewer was approved or available. The provenance and outcome-exposure ledger rule out presenting this analysis as untouched confirmation.

A strong methods submission would need: (1) a truly new independent cohort or a prospective source-only boundary; (2) qualified blinded event annotation with inter-rater reliability; (3) registered sigma and other time-frequency baselines at identical time budgets; (4) comparator methods with their own exact or explicit nonexact faithfulness guarantees; (5) well-defined missing-information and physical-intervention estimands; (6) uncertainty at the person level; and (7) evidence of practical value beyond cheaper signal summaries. Patentability and world-first novelty are **not assessed or claimed**.
