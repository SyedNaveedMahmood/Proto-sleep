# Phase XIII single-channel morphology codebook v1

Status: literature-grounded operational draft, ready for qualified review; not a validated clinical scoring manual. The user confirmed that no approved qualified reviewers are available. No human event labels or agreement statistics exist for this phase. Epoch sleep stages are not event annotations. The original data use R&K stages with3/4 merged; this morphology vocabulary does not silently relabel those stages under a different convention.

| Category | Observable candidate and interval marking | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Spindle-like | Discrete rhythmic roughly11–16Hz burst, commonly waxing/waning, sustained about≥.5s; mark visible burst onset/offset | Central maxima and multilead confirmation unavailable; sigma power alone is not a spindle |
| K-complex-like | Distinct biphasic sharp slow transient, with negative/positive deflections and total duration about≥.5s; mark the visible complex | Fpz-Cz polarity/reference differs from standard derivations; a large transient need not be a K-complex |
| Slow-wave-like | Visibly slow oscillatory activity, approximately.5–2Hz; mark recognizable cycles or continuous runs, record uncertainty | Clinical frontal amplitude/stage-percentage criteria do not transfer automatically to this bipolar lead; no automatic75uV diagnosis |
| Alpha-like | Rhythmic approximately8–13Hz activity; mark observable runs | Occipital dominance and eye-opening reactivity cannot be established from Fpz-Cz; does not prove Wake |
| Theta-like | Approximately4–7Hz rhythmic or mixed activity; mark visible runs | Nonspecific, not an independent N1 diagnosis |
| Sharp transient | Abrupt sharply contoured visible feature; mark extent without naming a clinical event | Vertex maxima, epileptiform classification and movement source cannot be established here |
| Broad spectral pattern | Observable sustained low/mixed/high-frequency pattern; mark its interval and describe it | It is a waveform description, not a stage or artifact label |
| Possible artifact | Plausible abrupt baseline jump, clipping, flat segment, movement-like burst or high-frequency contamination | Large amplitude/high frequency alone does not prove artifact; EOG/EMG unavailable |
| Mixed pattern | Overlapping or inconsistent morphology; mark interval and explain uncertainty | Stage-transition ambiguity is not proven by a model/scored-label mismatch |

Every category supports present, absent, uncertain, not assessable, possible artifact and insufficient signal quality. Absence requires a defensible negative observation in an assessable signal; inability to confirm from this lead is not absence. Confidence is0–3 (no confidence/low/moderate/high), independent of visibility and quality flags. Multiple overlapping categories are allowed. Present intervals use half-open samples[0,3000], displayed in seconds at100Hz. No automatic detector populates annotations.

Only Fpz-Cz is authorized. No additional EEG leads, EOG, chin EMG, respiration or other channels are exposed. REM-eye movements, chin atonia, standard multilead topography and full arousal criteria cannot be assessed. Same-recording adjacent physical context is optional and label-free; exact context/display changes are logged. Scorer uncertainty, signal quality and possible artifact remain separate fields.

Primary-source basis: [AASM scoring manual resource](https://aasm.org/clinical-resources/scoring-manual/) identifies the normative resource; full manual content was not accessible in this session and no claim of licensed manual reproduction is made. [Wendt et al., inter-/intra-expert spindle reliability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4426257/) reports imperfect expert agreement; retrieved indexed excerpts support maintaining uncertainty rather than constructing a single unquestioned label. [Warby et al., expert and nonexpert spindle detection](https://sleepcohort.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1452/2020/10/Warby2014.pdf) motivates independent event matching and an explicit review protocol; direct full text was access-restricted, so only indexed primary-source excerpts were reviewed. [Single-channel spindle scorer discrepancies](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sleep/articles/10.3389/frsle.2024.1427540/full) illustrates sensitivity to morphology and frequency conventions. [Japanese Society of Sleep Research PSG scoring chart](https://jssr.jp/files/download/PSGchartE.pdf) is an additional scoring reference, not authority to infer unavailable modalities. Qualified reviewers must review this operational draft before genuine scoring.

Clinical-event absence, exact morphology boundaries and labeling reliability remain untested. Generated examples exercise the software only; they are never real human annotations or proof of clinical validity.
