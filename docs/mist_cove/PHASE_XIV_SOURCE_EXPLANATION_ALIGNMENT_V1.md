# Phase XIV: SC source explanation and signal alignment

The historical P3B `raw_context`/123 checkpoint was byte authenticated and restricted loaded. Its additive availability operator retains exact full-input identity. The SC audit reuses the fixed 150 stage-balanced Phase XIII examples and separately samples 150 prevalence examples. It constructs 900 complete 15-atom response tables, each with all 32,768 states; all finite diagrams and displayed signed/positive clauses reconstruct their source response tables. It used 29,493,900 cached head responses, not that many full waveform network forwards.

Of 150 balanced cases, 122 were predicted correctly. Eighty have a displayed compact active clause under the registered eight-second/eight-literal display limit; 36 require all 30 seconds for the minimum positive support and 27 retain the target at zero local availability. In 126 cases, adding an atom can remove the response. Mean reduced diagram complexity is 479 nonterminal nodes. The displayed clauses cover a mean 0.471 of positive table states. This exact internal validity is independent of whether the prediction is correct or whether any retained interval contains a sleep event.

The prespecified proxy gate uses the five developmental people, the Pz-Oz spindle-like candidate intervals, and support selected on Fpz-Cz. Only nine event-eligible nonempty cases from four people survive; the required denominator is at least 20 cases and five people. Hence both SC proxy gates are **NOT ESTIMABLE**. The exact-minus-length-matched-random participant mean is +0.0208, bootstrap 95% interval [−0.00125, 0.06375]. The exact-minus-sigma-ranked mean is −0.0250, interval [−0.075, 0]. These are descriptive, not evidence of a passing alignment gate. An all-15-person audit and the prevalence panel remain labeled exploratory and separate.

Every comparator uses its registered duration/atom budget: matched random, position, RMS, spectral band, gradient and greedy's own budget. Timing controls shift proxy intervals by ten seconds or permute the Pz-Oz epoch within the same recording. Overlap is the fraction of selected time in an automated proxy, not clinical precision. Zero, 0.1 and 0.25 second tolerances were frozen before outcomes; `proxy_tolerance_sensitivity.json` reports their cached results. Correct and wrong predictions are retained separately; the four proxy-eligible wrong development examples cannot support a robust correct-versus-wrong claim.

Internal withholding, neutral replacement and physical waveform replacement give different answers. At neutral 0.01 versus exact zero, 127/150 tables and 34/150 minimum positive supports change. Mild HF leaves the prediction stable in 142/150 cases but preserves the same displayed clause in only 119/150; five other original active clauses can substitute. Forty-two of 2,250 SC bridge requests pass the historical compatibility guard, and none of 2,250 background requests are attempted because no eligible donor meets the policy. Ten compatible bridge comparisons disagree with the structural response test. Physical target removal, non-target preservation, waveform compatibility and clinical event deletion are distinct claims. Clinical deletion and human event agreement are **NOT TESTED**.
