# Prospective reliability-routing comparison v1

Decision: GO for one bounded mechanism experiment, not a claim that a replacement classifier is ready. Original-model primary accuracy was .66, .30 under the frozen global high-frequency probe, and .58 under a separately frozen single-region high-frequency follow-up. The participant-clustered local-probe accuracy difference was −.08, 95% descriptive bootstrap [−.1267, −.0267]. This supports testing whether class-blind local reliability can reduce sensitivity to known injected contamination. The sleep label remaining valid under injection is an assumption, not independently verified physiology.

Alternatives considered: preserve the historical model with post-hoc rejection; remove global inputs; use median/trimmed pooling; or add a small local reliability gate. Calibration alone cannot recover lost waveform information. Removing all global information would confound valid context with shortcuts. Fixed robust aggregation discards potentially diagnostic extremes without distinguishing their cause. We select one transparent gate and test it against a matched ungated control. This selection used original TRAIN only. No architecture search or extra seeds are authorized.

The Reliability-Routed Witness Ledger has fifteen nonoverlapping 200-sample local regions, an encoder shared across regions (Conv1d 1→8, kernel9/stride2, ELU; Conv1d 8→16, kernel5/stride2, ELU; mean pooling), and a bias-free 16→5 signed evidence head bounded by 3 tanh. Its scalar, class-blind reliability head is 4→8→1 with ELU/sigmoid, using log centered RMS, high-frequency power fraction, absolute DC, and peak/RMS. A separately reported global head 7→16→5 consumes log epoch RMS, DC and five spectral fractions. All signal statistics are features, not diagnosed artifacts.

Logits satisfy exactly, at the evaluated FP32 operation order,

\[\ell_c=b_c+g_c+\frac1{15}\sum_j r_j e_{jc}.\]

M0 fixes r=1 (1,029 trainable parameters); M1 learns r with classification only; M2 adds the single objective below (M1/M2: 1,078 trainable parameters). All arms have the same 1,078 stored parameter slots; M0's unused gate is frozen. Same seeds give identical shared initial tensors. The intended architectural difference is routing; no claim of perfectly matched capacity with the historical 33,957-trainable-parameter model is made.

Each fit receives the same paired examples: clean x and x′ with a 35-Hz sinusoid of amplitude3 times centered epoch RMS in one uniformly chosen 200-sample atom, random phase, no change outside that atom. M0/M1 use .5[CE(x,y)+CE(x′,y)]. M2 adds, with coefficient1,

\[L_{local}=\operatorname{mean}_{j,c}(r'_je'_{jc}-\operatorname{stopgrad}[(1-m_j)r_je_{jc}])^2.\]

Here m identifies the generated corruption location, never a clinical event. For this strictly local encoder, untouched contributions are identical by construction, so their loss is zero: the effective training pressure suppresses signed contributions from the injected region. This limitation and possible over-suppression of weak diagnostic morphology are explicit. The objective is local contribution suppression under a physical injection; it is not Phase IX masked-query response consistency or full-logit consistency. The global branch is unconstrained by this objective and may remain nuisance-sensitive. Gating collapse and excess global reliance are failure outcomes.

Related work limits novelty: [mixtures of local experts](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/mixex.html) established learned gates; [attention MIL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/ilse18a.html) established learned instance aggregation and discusses instance-level interpretability limitations; [SelectiveNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html) established trained rejection; [Dynamask](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/crabbe21a.html) uses fitted dynamic perturbation masks for time-series importance; [robust federated aggregation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.13445) studies geometric-median aggregation in a different corruption setting. Our arithmetic gate, signed sum and corruption supervision are not individually novel. The tested contribution is this narrowly specified EEG mechanism experiment and operator audit, with novelty priority unresolved. No physiological-event localization or external clinical validity follows.
