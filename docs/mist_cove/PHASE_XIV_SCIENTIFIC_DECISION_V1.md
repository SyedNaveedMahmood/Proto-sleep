# MIST-COVE Phase XIV: scientific decision

**Decision.** The authenticated original model supports exact internal witness circuits and retains a minimum level of frozen cross-study ST classification. The biological alignment claim is narrower: an ST spindle-like proxy overlap beats duration-matched random intervals in 25 eligible cases from 12 people, yet loses to a matched sigma-spectrum explanation. The SC developmental proxy gate is not estimable, and the six-fit context intervention fails its transition gate. REM recognition and calibration deteriorate strongly on ST. Human expert event validity and clinical interpretation remain **NOT TESTED**. Do not promote the context candidate, claim causal event localization, tune on ST, or begin another phase from these outcomes.

## Answers to the 30 preregistered decision questions

1. **Expected root files?** Yes. The exact authorized root contains all allowed 29 SC and 44 ST original PSG/hypnogram pairs, plus the explicitly approved root metadata and condition spreadsheet. No alternate root or cohort was searched.
2. **Independent integrity?** Yes. All 146 EDF files passed the official SHA256 references and strict paired geometry. The SC originals also match preserved historical references. Failed/quarantined pairs: zero.
3. **Observed channels and sample rates?** Both EEG Fpz-Cz and Pz-Oz and horizontal EOG are 100 Hz in both studies. SC chin EMG is a 1 Hz processed envelope; ST chin EMG is 100 Hz raw. SC Event marker is 1 Hz. The observed ST Marker is 10 Hz with native `ID+M-E`, contrary to a generic 1 Hz description. Respiration and temperature were inventoried and excluded from waveform analysis.
4. **Reproducible event proxies?** Fixed stage-blind YASA 0.6.5 spindle and slow-wave detectors, spectral controls, EOG excursions, native-rate EMG summaries and artifact flags completed on every approved recording with zero execution failures. Generated positive/flat fixtures and filter controls pass. Reproduction means deterministic execution under this sealed environment, not expert event accuracy.
5. **Spindle cross-channel agreement?** Default pooled F1 is 0.5601 on SC (12,945 matched among 21,596 Fpz-Cz and 24,632 Pz-Oz candidates) and 0.6698 on ST (29,894 matched among 46,191 and 43,078). Stricter RMS settings yield 0.5714 and 0.6725, respectively.
6. **Slow-wave cross-channel agreement?** Default pooled F1 is 0.0643 SC and 0.2764 ST. Relaxed-amplitude settings yield 0.1381 SC and 0.3709 ST. These weak and setting-sensitive results constrain any slow-wave localization claim.
7. **Usable EOG and EMG?** EOG 0.3–5 Hz excursions and RMS are descriptive error covariates. SC uses the native 1 Hz chin envelope; ST uses 100 Hz raw chin signal filtered to 20–45 Hz plus derived RMS. Their values are not directly pooled. Neither is an expert event reference.
8. **Support versus random?** On the prespecified Pz-Oz spindle-like endpoint, the SC five-person development subset is underpowered: nine eligible cases/four people; exact minus matched random +0.0208, 95% participant bootstrap [−0.00125, 0.06375], gate **NOT ESTIMABLE**. ST has 25 eligible cases/12 people; +0.1168 [0.0466, 0.2121], gate **PASS**. Overlap is selected-time proxy overlap, not event precision.
9. **Versus amplitude and spectral controls?** ST exact support exceeds matched RMS on the selected primary cases, but falls behind matched sigma by −0.0647 [−0.1312, −0.0124]; the predefined spectral-increment gate **FAILS**. SC minus sigma is −0.0250 [−0.075, 0], with inadequate eligibility. Exact mathematical faithfulness does not establish stronger physiological localization than simple spectra.
10. **Correct versus wrong?** Both are explained for the model's own predicted stage. ST has 103 correct and 116 wrong sampled cases; exact positive Pz-Oz spindle proxy overlap in eligible strata is descriptively 0.0663 versus 0.2407. Eligibility and stage composition differ (9 versus 16 cases), so greater overlap on wrong cases cannot be interpreted as better stage reasoning. SC developmental wrong eligibility is only four cases.
11. **Exact signed and positive explanations?** Yes, all 900 SC original-model and 1,095 ST original-model full 15-atom tables reconstruct all 32,768 states each; displayed signed and positive clauses pass exact verification. The context operator adds 60 complete 17-variable tables, 131,072 states each. This proves internal operator behavior only.
12. **Neutral-state sensitivity?** Changing hidden-feature neutral from zero to 0.01 changes 127/150 SC and 206/219 ST primary response tables and changes minimum positive supports in 34 and 45 cases. Operator choice is material; no unique waveform-causal explanation is claimed.
13. **Physical versus structural disagreement?** Of compatible physical bridges, 10/42 SC and 102/500 ST comparisons disagree with the corresponding structural class-loss test. ST background disagreement is 7/94 compatible replacements. SC background had zero attempted compatible donors. Guard rejection rates, non-target exactness and target response are reported separately. Clinical event deletion is **NOT TESTED**.
14. **Original model on ST?** ST scored-grid accuracy is 0.6987, pooled macro-F1 0.5839 and mean person macro-F1 0.5583 [0.5328, 0.5842]. The frozen minimal transfer gate **PASSES**. The SC in-sample counterparts are 0.9477, 0.8246 and 0.8160.
15. **Which stages deteriorated?** Recall falls N1 0.5044→0.2809, N3 0.8690→0.7790 and REM 0.7914→0.2162; N2 remains 0.9207→0.9058. Wake recall also falls 0.9942→0.9089. REM is the clearest recognition failure.
16. **Confidence and calibration?** ST ECE15 is 0.1339 versus SC 0.0030; negative log likelihood 0.9149 versus 0.1376 and Brier 0.4338 versus 0.0739. High-confidence wrong predictions are 3,209/42,691 ST scored epochs, versus 433/79,025 SC. No ST recalibration was performed.
17. **ST conditions?** In 22 authenticated within-person pairs, temazepam minus placebo accuracy is +0.0426 [0.0224, 0.0639], macro-F1 +0.0252 [−0.0028, 0.0512], N1 recall +0.0450 [0.0060, 0.0777]. Candidate spindle rate is +68.54/hour. These many descriptive associations are not causal medication effects.
18. **Acquisition confounds?** SC versus ST differ in EMG type/rate and marker rate, and also potentially montage amplitude, night selection, medication and wake prevalence. Unpaired participant contrasts cannot identify which factor causes model or proxy differences.
19. **Proxy distribution shift?** Participant mean Fpz-Cz spindle candidate rates are 32.48/hour SC versus 121.87/hour ST; Pz-Oz 36.38 versus 113.99. Fpz-Cz slow-wave candidates are 145.30 versus 167.20; Pz-Oz 15.80 versus 54.11. Stage-balanced EEG centered RMS is +20.73 µV on ST [17.21, 24.09] and sigma fraction +0.00864 [0.00361, 0.01413]. All are automated/acquisition-sensitive measurements.
20. **Compactness and stability transfer?** Displayed compact active clauses are 80/150 SC and 98/219 ST, with whole-epoch positive minima 36 and 53. Mean displayed-clause state coverage is 0.471 SC versus 0.304 ST; mean BDD nodes 479 versus 658. ST mild-HF leaves 208/219 predictions stable but only 142/219 same clauses valid. Sampling policies differ, so these are descriptive cross-study comparisons.
21. **Context rationale?** Prior Phase XIII results left transition-adjacent errors and saturation unresolved. A prespecified matched C0/C1 test isolated access to previous/next physical epochs at equal 11,974 stored parameters, equal training exposures and frozen SC only. C1 is offline because it uses future context.
22. **Context transition outcome?** C1 minus C0 transition accuracy is −0.015625, −0.046875 and −0.03125 for seeds 123/456/789. The gate required +0.03 in every seed along with F1, N1 and class-loss criteria. It **FAILS** in all seeds; no model was promoted from ST results.
23. **Context dependency complexity?** The independent 17-variable operator accounts for previous whole 30 seconds, fifteen center two-second atoms and next whole 30 seconds. C1 has active neighbor dependencies in 14/15 selected five-case-by-three-seed checks, C0 in 0/15. All exact tables verify. The additional complexity has no qualifying transition benefit in this tested family.
24. **Detector sensitivity?** ST spindle exact-minus-random remains positive across the fixed 0/0.1/0.25-second tolerances (+0.1168/+0.133/+0.152), and stricter spindle detection retains a positive descriptive contrast. Exact-minus-sigma remains negative (−0.0647/−0.078/−0.087). SC lacks sufficient eligible development cases. Slow-wave variants disagree strongly, so the spindle result cannot be generalized across proxy families. The first cached tolerance summary accidentally included unassessable flat cases; its digest and correction are preserved in `sensitivity_status_filter_correction.json`. Primary gates were unaffected.
25. **Missing human evidence?** No approved qualified reviewers were available. Expert agreement, clinically meaningful events, true event deletion, patient-level usefulness and clinical staging validity remain **NOT TESTED**. The private atlas is labeled automated proxies only.
26. **Strongest contribution?** A reproducible, authenticated pipeline jointly checks exact internal response circuits, physical replacement guards and independently computed channel/event proxies on original SC and frozen ST data, while retaining disagreements and negative outcomes. It gives a falsifiable map of model behavior, not priority or clinical-causality proof.
27. **Clinical interpretation requirements?** Qualified blinded experts must review original multichannel waveforms and event candidates under a prospective protocol; agreement, adjudication, calibration, stage-error utility and operator-specific event deletion need independent estimates. Such reviewers were not available this phase.
28. **Independent generalization requirements?** A separately authorized cohort with locked model, detectors, thresholds and event-label adjudication is needed. Sleep-EDF SC and ST are distinct study components but do not establish general population transfer; no SHHS or RESERVED access is implied.
29. **Falsified assumptions?** The tested context candidate does not improve SC transition accuracy; the ST explanation does not outperform matched sigma ranking; slow-wave candidates do not agree well across two EEG views; prediction stability does not imply original-clause stability; source-like calibration does not transfer. The SC five-person physiological alignment gate is too sparse to decide.
30. **Single highest-value next experiment?** A separately approved, blinded expert review of a preregistered, balanced SC/ST multichannel event-and-error panel, with the existing detectors and model held fixed. It would directly test whether automated proxies measure the biological phenomena required for clinical interpretation. Human validation remains **NOT TESTED** until that review occurs.

## Methods, novelty and limits

The response circuit combines established sufficient-subset reasoning, fixed-order binary decision diagrams, signed finite response clauses and time-series interventions. Related foundations include [sufficient input subsets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03805), [time-series deletion masks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/crabbe21a.html), [binary decision diagrams](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~bryant/pubdir/ieeetc86.pdf), and [abductive explanations](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/3964). Its contribution here is the audited combination of authenticated EEG input availability, physical and structural operator comparison, independent automated signal checks and frozen cross-study testing. Neither first-in-world novelty nor clinical validity follows. The spindle/slow-wave proxies draw on [YASA documentation](https://yasa-sleep.org/generated/yasa.spindles_detect.html), [YASA slow-wave documentation](https://yasa-sleep.org/generated/yasa.sw_detect.html), [Lacourse and colleagues](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6415669/) and [Massimini and colleagues](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/24/31/6862.full.pdf), but the sealed implementation is not a full reproduction of any expert standard. The 0.6.5 installed API and fixed parameters govern this experiment.

The full CPU regression suite passed **1,139 tests**, skipped 11 CUDA tests and emitted seven warnings in its recorded run; the final post-correction suite also passed on the same collected tests. The final exact-commit CI result is attached to the draft PR externally. CUDA correctness was not run under the CPU-only patched runtime. Successful real-audit head operations total **73,241,181**: 29,493,900 SC, 35,882,931 ST, 7,864,350 context. A separately counted generated-proof lower bound is 589,826; inherited regression synthetic calls were not individually instrumented, so an exact all-test query total is not certified. Six fits/18,000 core updates satisfy their ceilings. The conservative sum of stage walls, including recorded engineering failures, is in `resource_receipts.json` and remains far below 24,000 seconds. Parent-process peak RSS is instrumented; detector subprocess peak is not.

One engineering privacy failure is material: an earlier pushed Phase XIV source commit contained the user-supplied dataset root literal. The latest tree replaces it with a digest-pinned ignored local authorization file and passes its release scan. The earlier commit remains in branch history; history was not rewritten. No EEG waveforms, individual prediction rows, private atlas, checkpoints or fitted statistics were published. This disclosure limits any claim of a wholly path-private branch history. Other preserved failures: a generated flat-control null serialization problem, an isolated detector-worker import conflict before first ST event output, and a continuation entry collision before subsequent ST value reads. The first ST predictions were retained and reused. Corrections changed transport/aggregation only, not model, detector thresholds, sample ranks, primary gates or ST candidate selection.
