# Phase VIII future source-only proposal v1 — proposal only

No real data loader is invoked by Phase VIII. A future source study requires a separate explicit human authorization and independent review of synthetic prerequisites. Existing 15 TRAIN / 4 VAL / 55 RESERVED participants stay frozen. Original calibrated Fpz-Cz EEG, 100 Hz and 3,000 samples per 30-second physical epoch are mandatory. Retain every complete unscored physical position and mask, original night/epoch coordinates, TRAIN-only normalization and waveform banks. No trimmed legacy NPZ substitution, VAL-fitted scaling/donors, RESERVED, ST, SHHS or adaptation is proposed.

The same four-person source validation has been repeatedly used for research development and checkpoint selection. It is not an independent external test. Even future source improvement would not establish clinical event detection, diagnostic validity or external generalization.

## Prerequisites before reviewing a launch

1. One fixed blind representation satisfies all robustness, clean-penalty and paired-use clauses in every synthetic task/seed, including fresh artifact/overlap families under a new prospective identity.
2. Calibrated uncertainty and diagnostic-removal behavior are adequate under declared endpoints, without replacing classification with selective reporting.
3. If the objective concerns explanations, the complete successful fixed-encoder E0/E1/E2/E3 comparison shows retained classification and incremental localization/component-effect benefit. Stable ranking/accounting alone is insufficient.
4. Independent acceptable, diagnostically neutral synthetic donors have adequate coverage and stable conditional effects; real donor fitting remains a distinct TRAIN-only authorization.
5. Source fingerprints, exact CPU/CUDA resume and measured bounded GPU feasibility pass on the future implementation. Prior-phase receipts cannot be reused as new proof.
