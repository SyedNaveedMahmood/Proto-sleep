# Phase VIII scientific hypotheses v1

H8.1: Class-independent high-energy artifacts perturb local/pooled features and create confident errors. Matched taxonomy/strength curves and exact arithmetic can support susceptibility, but cannot uniquely identify physiological causality. Falsifier: no paired degradation or feature response in the tested regimes.

H8.2 (primary): B1 balanced exposure meets the complete robustness prerequisite in every task/seed: clean F1 >=.80, clean F1 no more than .03 below P0, original combined competitive and added high-frequency F1 >=.70, clean/competitive appropriate class swaps >=.80, nuisance replacement agreement >=.80, no >=98% class collapse. Any clause or missing seed fails the collective gate. No rounding or candidate substitution.

H8.3: A1 artifact-focused exposure improves artifact F1 over B1 without a clean loss >.03 in all six pairs. This is secondary, not a replacement primary. A gain on one family is not universal robustness.

H8.4: T1 trimmed candidate aggregation improves original competitive F1 by >=.03 versus matched B1 in all six pairs without clean loss >.03. Fixed sorting drops the two highest and lowest candidates independently per feature. It can erase sparse diagnostics; failure is scientifically plausible.

H8.5: Robustness training reduces corrupted NLL/Brier and incorrect >=.9 confidence versus P0 in every pair. Temperature scaling is a separate descriptive baseline fit only on generated calibration data. Classification gains need not imply calibration gains.

H8.6: Mean confidence after complete component deletion decreases by >=.10 from intact competitive inputs in every primary pair. Selective risk/coverage is descriptive, with a calibration-fit 80% acceptance policy. This does not assert clinical safety or provable epistemic uncertainty.

H8.7: Conditional matched-head study only after H8.2 PASS: exact shared frozen encoder, K4, E0/E1/E2/E3; E1 must retain clean/competitive F1 within .03 of E0 and improve competitive top-K hit by .10 over E3 with positive matched diagnostic-minus-control component effect. Otherwise NOT TESTED. Exact accounting alone is insufficient.

H8.8: Independently acceptable synthetic donors provide measurable conditional prediction agreement/effect spread. Zero or fewer than two accepted donors per example yields NOT TESTED for stability on that example. Numerical acceptance cannot establish physiological neutrality.

All intervals concern generated draws or optimization seeds. Three seeds are not three people. Correlated transformations share a draw-cluster ID. No multiplicity-adjusted population significance or new clinical claims are proposed.
