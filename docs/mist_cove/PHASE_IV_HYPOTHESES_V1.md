# Phase IV hypotheses v1 — synthetic mechanisms, not clinical transfer

Phase III is a completed negative experiment. q1/availability/background/rounded_q1 source-VAL means were 0.477645/0.487475/0.354091/0.478864, versus raw_context 0.723511 and evidence 0.718031. H1 superiority failed earlier; individual event necessity, donor robustness and clinical waveform recognition remain unestablished. These historical files/results are immutable. This phase permits generated data and mocked interfaces only, without new TRAIN/VAL EEG, RESERVED descriptors, ST or SHHS access.

**H4.1 representation.** A small temporal encoder can learn generated phase/morphology codes beyond five-band/RMS statistics. Prediction: end-to-end M1 exceeds both unchanged M0 and same-capacity phase-removed M1 by ≥0.10 fixed-five F1 on clean and competitive fixtures, in every seed of the substitutable task. Falsifier: either margin fails. The phase control preserves each input patch's entire DFT magnitude, mean and RMS, but independently reconstructed overlapping patches need not form a realizable continuous waveform. Cropping can expose power differences; a successful phase control falsifies the distinguishing comparison. Nothing establishes real clinical events.

**H4.2 organization.** Given identical learned encoder weights, sparse additive evidence improves useful evidence reporting without excessive classification cost. Primary fixed-encoder comparison: initialize M1/M2/M3 from that seed/task's TRAIN-only final M1 encoder, freeze it, reset heads and fit all heads for the same 150 updates. M1's pretraining head is discarded; its representation provenance is common to all heads. Prediction in every task/seed: M2 clean F1 ≥ M1−0.03, competitive top-ranked localization ≥ M1 internal feature-deletion localization+0.10 and ≥0.80, and selected-waveform true-stage effect exceeds a predeclared non-diagnostic control by ≥0.05. Donor effect spread must be ≤0.25 logits for an unqualified witness claim. Falsify on any failed clause. M1 feature deletion is explicitly an internal diagnostic, distinct from waveform replacement. End-to-end comparisons are secondary and do not hold learned weights fixed.

**H4.3 plural evidence.** Useful selected waves can be substitutable without individual necessity. On redundant/correlated fixtures, report individual effects, joint generator-declared group effects, background and reselection separately under oracle component subtraction, bridge and independent background erasure. Prediction for M2: redundant joint absolute true-stage effect exceeds largest individual by ≥0.25, tiny-noise top-set Jaccard distance ≤0.10, and wholly erased mean confidence ≤0.50 in every task/seed. Donor spread ≤0.25 is separately required for donor robustness. Failures remain visible; group dependence does not imply independently established physiological redundancy. Complementary one-factor loss is symmetric and deliberately ambiguous.

**Distractor mechanism.** Compare M2 learned stage-contrast top-K with M2_rms: identical encoder/head/parameters/budget, different ranking only. M2 must improve competitive F1 and top-ranked localization by ≥0.10 in every task/seed. Larger peaks alone must not pass. Negative results are valid; no architecture, threshold, generator or budget is changed to rescue them.

Three seeds repeat generated optimization experiments, not participants. Statistical generalization, real EEG accuracy, external transfer, clinical identity and physiological donor validity are NOT TESTED. No real training authorization is created by software or synthetic success.
