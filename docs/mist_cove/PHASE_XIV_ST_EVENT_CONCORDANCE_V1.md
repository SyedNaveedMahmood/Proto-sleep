# Phase XIV: ST automated event concordance

The 44 authenticated ST recordings completed stage-blind proxy detection with zero detector execution failures. The same sealed detector rules and calibrated EEG/EOG channels used on SC were applied to each complete physical recording. ST raw chin EMG is 100 Hz, so its 20–45 Hz measure is acquisition-distinct from the SC 1 Hz envelope. The ST marker is measured at 10 Hz and is not decoded as a clinical event label.

Spindle-like Fpz-Cz/Pz-Oz candidates number 46,191/43,078, with 29,894 matched and pooled cross-channel F1 **0.6698**. The strict-RMS sensitivity F1 is 0.6725. Slow-wave-like candidates number 62,835/20,255, with 11,482 matched and F1 **0.2764**; relaxed amplitude gives 0.3709. Agreement rises relative to SC but remains poor for the slow-wave proxy. Correlated channel agreement cannot substitute for independent expert annotation.

The ST witness panel is prospectively sampled one scored epoch per stage, person and night, capped at 220. One stratum is absent; **219 cases** from all 22 people are retained without replacement. Each case has five complete response tables (base, mild HF, drift, gain and neutral 0.01), all 32,768 states, yielding **1,095 verified tables**. Ninety-eight cases display a compact active clause, 53 have a whole-epoch positive minimum, six are prior-only, and 202 have at least one nonmonotone edge. Mean BDD complexity is 657.6 nonterminal nodes; displayed clause coverage averages 0.304 of positive states. Accurate finite explanation remains possible for incorrect stage predictions.

For the registered Pz-Oz spindle-like endpoint, 25 cases from 12 people have an eligible event and nonempty support. Exact retained time exceeds matched random by **+0.1168** participant mean (95% bootstrap [0.0466, 0.2121]), so that minimum proxy gate passes. It trails matched sigma ranking by **−0.0647** (interval [−0.1312, −0.0124]); the incremental spectral gate fails. Twenty-five eligible cases are a small subset of 219, and this proxy match is not clinical event accuracy. Shift/permutation, detector setting and time-tolerance results are reported as controls, not post-hoc confirmation.

The same original clause survives mild HF in 142/219 cases, despite 208/219 stable predictions; drift 182/219 versus 216/219; gain 208/219 versus 212/219. One mild-HF case has a different valid original active clause. Predictions and explanations must be reported separately. No qualified human review was available; expert validity remains **NOT TESTED**.
