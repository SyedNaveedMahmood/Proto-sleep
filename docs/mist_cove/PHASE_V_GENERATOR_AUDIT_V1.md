# Phase V historical generator and information audit v1

This is a new audit of frozen Phase IV v2, not a replacement of its results. Historical model/generator/config/preregistration/publication/CUDA-addendum files passed their original byte-hash checks. Instrumented replay matches the original generator bit-for-bit in twelve task/condition unit cases. It retains the original order of floating-point additions and random draws; nuisance-only inputs are constructed separately rather than recovered through potentially class-dependent cancellation roundoff.

## Source-level findings

`phase4.synthetic.templates` draws nine fixed phase codes on a 200-sample real-DFT grid, shares one frequency amplitude envelope and one RMS scaling, and uses five diagnostic codes plus four separate nuisance codes. The diagnostic code changes with the assigned label; nuisance identities do not. The balanced label permutation and later nuisance draws share one NumPy generator, while Gaussian noise uses a separately instantiated Torch generator with the same integer seed. There is no class-identity-dependent random branch or count. The substitutable TRAIN event count is random and class-free. Complementary labels deliberately select adjacent code identities; that relation is intended diagnostic information.

The replay records every diagnostic code, phase-bank reference, amplitude, onset/duration/count, nuisance identity/amplitude/position, background amplitude/phase, noise stream seed/offset, generation order, overlap and cropping geometry. Complete draw records remain private and generated-only. Swapping the diagnostic label while replaying the RNG leaves the nuisance tensor and every class-free latent exactly unchanged. Reverse pairs keep the diagnostic tensor unchanged and replace only independently generated nuisance. These checks do not prove all possible finite-decoder independence claims; they isolate the declared causal variables.

`MorphModel.encode_candidates` extracts fourteen 400-sample patches at stride200. M1_phase separately centers and zero-phase reconstructs each patch, restores its mean, and then applies the same encoder. Original overlapping samples are therefore assigned multiple inconsistent reconstructed values. This is a valid deterministic internal representation, but it is not one physical waveform on the original grid and is not a physiological intervention.

## Measured mechanisms

The five length-200 templates' maximum relative magnitude class range is **2.17e-7**, compatible with float32 construction roundoff. Common RMS differences are at most **2.01e-8**. However, zero padding alone exposes a relative class range of **0.7114** at length400 and **0.7363** at length3000. Cropping to100 samples and evaluating at400 gives **0.7606**. Complete-event translation changes magnitude by only **3.52e-16** in the double-precision diagnostic. A translation retaining the complete event is not itself this leakage mechanism.

A restricted magnitude decoder on the zero-embedded diagnostic-only signal reaches clean accuracy **1.0000** on substitutable examples and **0.999333** on complementary examples. Every seed's 99% lower bound exceeds the frozen .30 criterion. No background or artifact is needed for that counterexample. Matching isolated DFT magnitudes did not match the interpolated spectrum of the embedded finite waveform.

In complete signals, diagnostic/nuisance spectral cross terms have absolute-power fraction means **0.2916 / 0.3024** across the two historical tasks. The normalized power-identity residual is below **8.44e-9**. These measurements verify interference is present; they do not quantify its unique causal share of classifier performance. Approximately 7,400 of 11,400 diagnostic-event/candidate intersections per seed contain a truncated view of the complete 200-sample event. That is window cropping, not truncation of the original physical waveform.

On newly generated clean complete inputs, epoch-magnitude accuracy is **0.4163 / 0.3025** and patch-magnitude accuracy **0.3638 / 0.2695**. Substitutable complete-epoch and patch probes satisfy the strong leakage criterion in all three seeds. Complementary complete-input probes fail that strong criterion and also fail near-chance equivalence: their result is intermediate, not proof of absent information. The diagnostic-only counterexample remains decisive for both tasks. All corresponding intervals and per-stage metrics are published.

Historical patchwise reconstruction produces overlap disagreement around **10.23–10.31 µV RMS**, versus exactly zero for original and coherent whole-epoch reconstruction. It preserves centered patch magnitudes to relative error below **1.15e-7** and imposes circular even symmetry. Coherent zero-phase reconstruction yields large end-to-start jumps, about64 µV RMS here versus about0.7 µV originally. Neither transformation is assumed label-preserving or physiologically valid.

## Explanations supported and unresolved

Position, nuisance-magnitude, nuisance-latent, amplitude-summary, envelope/boundary and permuted-label probes satisfy the declared clean near-chance interval criterion in all historical seeds. This provides no evidence of label leakage through those specified nuisance decoders. Their confidence can nevertheless be poor, and finite fixed-budget probes cannot exclude all nonlinear nuisance or RNG associations.

In the new matched-exposure audit, patchwise M1_phase beats both original M1 and coherent M1 by the frozen .10 F1 margin in every seed/task. Thus the unusual advantage persists under these controls, while phase removal at whole-epoch scale does not reproduce it. Successful reconstruction-free magnitude decoding and the zero-padding counterexample support spectral accessibility as an alternative to exclusive temporal-morphology learning. Artificial zero-phase symmetry and inconsistent overlaps are measured transformation effects. The study does not uniquely apportion the advantage among those effects, finite-model optimization, local phase–magnitude interactions and feature normalization. A deterministic transform cannot create label information absent from its input. The historical transform retains signed patch means as well as centered magnitudes, whereas the magnitude-only probes retain unsigned DC magnitude. Different information maps and finite decoders are additional limits on attributing the whole neural gap to reconstruction artifacts.

**Historical phase-isolation claim: FAIL.** Pure position/nuisance leakage through tested controls: **not supported within the prespecified sensitivity**, with broader absence unproved. A single exclusive explanation for the historical neural optimization gap: **NOT ESTABLISHED**. The original Phase IV negative findings remain unchanged.
