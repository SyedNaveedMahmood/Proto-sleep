Highest-priority unresolved questions: (1) Can an automatic representation retain weak local diagnostics under overlapping broadband/abrupt interference with no large clean penalty? Identifiable with matched fresh synthetic interventions. (2) Can robust aggregation retain sparse signals rather than remove them? Feasible and falsifiable, with no privileged gate inputs. (3) Do native signed witnesses add explanation utility once representation quality is adequate? High value but currently prerequisite-limited. (4) Can numerical donor coverage coexist with diagnostic neutrality and multi-donor stability? Feasible independently of model superiority. (5) Do clinically annotated events and independent populations support the same relationships? Highest external-validity importance, but least currently authorized/identified.

Representation capacity, receptive support, local/global context, pooling, exposure and confidence are interacting hypotheses. No completed result assigns all errors to a single cause. Avoid indiscriminate model growth, broad hyperparameter sweeps, repeated use of four VAL people as an independent test, or automatic Phase IX execution.
