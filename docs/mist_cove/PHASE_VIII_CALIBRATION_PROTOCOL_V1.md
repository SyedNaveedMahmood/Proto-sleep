# Phase VIII calibration and information loss protocol v1

For each of 36 reference/candidate task/seed combinations, fit one temperature on 500 independent generated calibration examples, using the B1 mixture and offset 8,400,000. Grid: 61 logarithmically spaced temperatures from 10^(-.6) to 10^(.8), earliest minimum cross-entropy. No locked examples fit a parameter. Fix the rejection threshold at the calibration distribution's 20th confidence percentile after scaling. Publish raw and scaled fixed-five metrics, 15 equal-width ECE bins (last includes 1), class-conditional confidence, error-confidence correlation and deterministic confidence-ranked coverage/risk at .1/.25/.5/.75/1. The fixed-threshold policy is distinct from the ranking curve.

Information-loss draws use offset 8,600,000 and 100 examples, retaining original labels as bookkeeping even where information is gone. Conditions: intact, .2 amplitude, half a single event or first member removed, all events removed, unrelated high-frequency replacement, high-energy nuisance retained, background/noise only. `removed` and `high_energy_remaining` deliberately share the same strong-competitive baseline; they are one intervention viewed under two questions, not independent observations. The partial-factor ordered-removal identifiability limitation from Phase VI remains.

Full deletion leaves the original label unrecoverable through diagnostic components, under the generator's class-independent baseline construction. A finite classifier's chance behavior is not a proof of total absence of all information. Uncalibrated and calibrated confidence, entropy, loss and selective risk remain useful if robustness fails. No new reliability endpoint unlocks the additive gate.
