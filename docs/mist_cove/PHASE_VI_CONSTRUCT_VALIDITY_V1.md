# Phase VI construct validity v1

**A: intended diagnostic mechanism.** A finite 200-sample tapered oscillatory component has an explicitly chosen code; Task A uses one complete class code. Task B uses either two copies of a complete class code or two complementary factor codes. The family of factor codes differs from the family of complete codes, so deleting one redundant witness can leave a fully identifying complete witness without confusing it with a partial factor. All families share duration, amplitude/phase distributions and insertion rules.

**B: legitimate diagnostic proxies.** Local magnitude spectra, extrema, energy and envelope can reveal those codes. Oracle-region amplitude/spectrum and full-magnitude decoders quantify them without being generator-invalidity tests. A successful spectral decoder narrows interpretation to local frequency/waveform information; no uniquely temporal phase claim is made. Knowing oracle intervals gives those probes additional information; their performance is not an architecture-fair comparison to blind full-waveform M1.

**C: unrelated shortcuts.** Background/noise, nuisance/artifact identity/amplitude, absolute insertion geometry, draw order and class-free RNG branches must not reveal the label. Separate SeedSequence streams and exact label-swapped pairs test the construction; independently evaluated background/nuisance/artifact/position probes test specified finite decoders. All labels are balanced independently of those streams. Reject or stop on failed/inconclusive equivalence rather than calling nonsignificance independence.

Localization is identifiable here because diagnostic support is 200/3000 samples in Task A and at most 400/3000 for the primary Task B. It is not global Phase V support. Candidate windows remain [j*200,j*200+400), j=0..13. Every complete 200-sample event is covered by at least one full candidate; other overlapping candidates can see fragments. The generator supplies intervals and predeclared groups only to auditors, never model.forward. Empty/removed inputs have empty oracle truth; retained labels are not claimed identifiable after complete deletion.

Oracle deletion recomposes the stored label-free baseline plus remaining components. It avoids claiming that floating-point x−d inverts rounded addition bitwise. Outside diagnostic support x equals the stored baseline exactly; deletion and paired nuisance preservation are exact under the declared composition. Internal score deletion and fixed-witness paths are model interventions, not physiological changes. Waveform donors modify boundaries/background and can be rejected independently of the oracle result.
