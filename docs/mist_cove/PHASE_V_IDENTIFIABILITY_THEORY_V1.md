These are algebraic mechanisms and constructed counterexamples, not empirical findings or clinical claims.

For DFT coefficients D and U of diagnostic and nuisance components, |D+U|² = |D|² + |U|² + 2 Re(D conj(U)). Holding |D| fixed does not hold the cross term fixed as diagnostic phase changes. Randomizing U can remove some conditional information in expectation without proving equivalence of the finite feature distributions seen by a classifier.

Equal magnitudes on a length-200 DFT grid constrain only those 101 real-signal frequency samples. Zero padding to 400/3000 evaluates the finite sequence's Fourier polynomial at additional frequencies. These intervening values need not agree between class codes. Thus embedding without nuisance can already expose magnitude information; this is distinct from interference with background. A temporal shift alone preserves the full zero-padded magnitude when the complete event is retained, but cropping changes the sequence and can expose additional differences. RMS equality alone says even less.

For a window w, F(w x) is the convolution of the signal spectrum and window spectrum. Local magnitudes can therefore depend on original temporal phase. Overlapping windows also share samples. Independently replacing each patch p with irFFT(|rFFT(p-mean(p))|)+mean(p) preserves its centered magnitude but generally gives different values to the same original sample in adjacent patches. An overlap disagreement larger than numerical error proves the transformed patch collection is not one waveform on the original physical grid. Zero-phase real reconstruction imposes circular even symmetry; DC and the even-length Nyquist bin become nonnegative magnitude coefficients. This is an internal information/optimization control, not a physiological counterfactual.

The coherent control performs that operation once at length 3000 and extracts patches afterwards. Real nonnegative rFFT coefficients define the conjugate-symmetric completion implicitly through irFFT, with backward normalization and no extra amplitude rescaling. The mean is restored. DC-centering roundoff and Nyquist sign removal are measured. End-to-start jumps are retained and measured, not hidden by filtering. Coherent phase removal need not preserve an artificial code's label, local magnitudes, or localization.

The new task uses x(t)=A[cos(2πft+θ)+0.7 cos(4πft+2θ+φ_y)]+U(t), φ_y=2πy/5, f=2 Hz. Every complete 30-second epoch and four-second candidate contains integer cycles. In the noiseless, no-nuisance case both full and candidate magnitudes are independent of y. For noiseless Fourier coefficients Z1,Z2, arg(Z2 conj(Z1)²)=φ_y modulo 2π, providing an analytic identifiability witness independent of nuisance global phase θ. This is an artificial harmonic relationship, not a sleep-stage biomarker. Additive stationary noise and a distinct integer-frequency nuisance do not justify asserting equality of all joint local magnitude distributions; the restricted-decoder audit remains necessary. Artifact localization/truncation may reintroduce class-dependent interference and is reported separately.

Restricted probes can establish information sufficiency when successful. Their failure cannot exclude every decoder, frequency statistic or optimization explanation. Near-chance equivalence is scoped to specified decoders, sample sizes, confidence bounds and conditions. A deterministic transform cannot create information about labels absent from its input; it can expose information more accessibly to a finite model or alter optimization. An advantage of patch reconstruction does not by itself identify a unique artificial cue. Seeds repeat generator/optimizer experiments, never human participants.
