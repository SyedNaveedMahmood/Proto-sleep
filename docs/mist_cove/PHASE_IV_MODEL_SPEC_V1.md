# Phase IV compact waveform model v1

Input is finite calibrated Fpz-Cz µV, 100 Hz, [B,1,3000]. The adapter reverses only supplied frozen TRAIN mean/std; it does not fit normalization. Four-second windows (400 samples), stride 200, produce fourteen candidate intervals, including both epoch endpoints. Every local representation depends only on its original half-open window. Overlapping candidates are permitted and never described as independent evidence.

The same encoder processes two channels per window: centered shape divided by its RMS (floor 0.1 µV), and original calibrated waveform /20 µV. Three Conv1d branches have four channels, kernels 9/25/75, stride 4 and symmetric padding; concatenate and apply 12→12 kernel 7 stride 4, then 12→16 kernel 5 stride 2, with GELU after each layer. Concatenate temporal mean/max, project 32→16 with GELU, and append log(1+physical centered RMS/10) and physical mean/10. Eighteen features preserve relative morphology and physical amplitude. The local receptive support is bounded by the full four-second patch; no global normalization or cross-epoch context is introduced.

M1 conventionally pools candidate-feature mean and max, then applies Linear(36,5). M2 uses Linear(18,5) background on the candidate mean plus a bias-free Linear(18,5) local head. Local signed scores are 8/K × tanh(raw stage scores), K=4. Ranking is the mean squared stage-centered raw local score: learned class contrast, not an observability estimate. Hard top-K uses stable physical-time ties, excludes numerically flat candidates, and allows overlap. M2_rms changes ranking only. M3 retains the encoder/background but disables the event head and its gradients. M1 and M2 have exactly the same declared head parameter count (185); effective encoder/head counts and gradients are measured separately. No attention, prototypes, MoE, reliability gate or event labels supervise training.

M1_phase uses the identical encoder/head on a deterministic zero-phase reconstruction of each centered patch, retaining its mean, DFT magnitude and RMS. This is an internal representation ablation with extra FFT cost, not a physiological counterfactual. M0 is the unchanged 75-parameter q1 with its original two-second nonoverlapping selector. It has a different representation/capacity and is not the sole distinguishing control.

M2 computes logits directly as background + sum signed contributions. Evidence includes original intervals, validity, rank indices/scores, local features, signed five-stage terms and physical waveform hash. Flat inputs have no fabricated events; low-information noise is not guaranteed to produce low confidence and is explicitly tested. Fixed witness identities/validity define an internal intervention even after evidence erasure. Individual/group full-waveform effects are separately recomputed; background/fixed-path/reselection reconcile to 1e-5. A ledger joins original epoch and raw-file provenance hashes separately from physical-input hashes. No metadata join authorizes source access.

Generated oracle subtraction removes known components without changing nuisance/noise and is the most controlled eraser. Bridge and independent background are additional donor-dependent interventions. Joint union bridges can change geometry relative to individual bridges, so nonadditivity does not isolate redundancy. Diagnostics expose continuity, energy, spectral change and unsupported donors. No finite/continuous replacement is certified physiological.
